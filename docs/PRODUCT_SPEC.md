CarWise UK — Product Specification

Brand

CarWise UK

Tagline: Buy smarter. Spend wiser. Drive with confidence.

Visual style: Luxury automotive black and gold.

Brand colours

- Black: #090909
- Graphite: #171717
- Gold: #D4AF37
- Warm white: #F2F0E9

Purpose

Help UK motorists make smarter car-buying decisions and understand the real cost of owning a car.

Main Features

1. Car Affordability Calculator

- Purchase price and deposit
- Finance amount, interest rate and term
- Insurance
- Road tax
- Mileage and fuel costs
- Maintenance and repairs
- Monthly and annual ownership costs

2. Used-Car Buying Checker

- Vehicle registration and details
- MOT history
- Mileage history
- MOT failures and advisories
- Service history
- Buying checklist
- Warning signs to investigate

3. Compare Cars

Compare two vehicles and their estimated ownership costs.

4. My Garage (Future Feature)

Save vehicles, calculations and reminders.

Initial Market

United Kingdom only.

Technology

- Next.js
- TypeScript
- Tailwind CSS
- GitHub
- Cloudflare Pages
- Supabase later if needed

Business Model

Initially free to use.

Possible future income:

- Affiliate partnerships
- Premium vehicle reports
- Premium features
- Advertising

Development Rules

- Keep costs as close to zero as possible.
- Build a working MVP first.
- Mobile-first responsive design.
- No paid APIs initially.
- Never invent vehicle or MOT information.
- Never guarantee a vehicle is safe to buy.
- Test everything before launch.

First Development Goal

Build a polished homepage, working affordability calculator, manual used-car checker and basic car comparison.
